Position & Frame Setup

Sit upright or stand facing your device camera (1.5 - 2 meters distance).

2

Controlled Flexion

Keep your upper arm stationary against your torso while bending the elbow upward.

3

Extension & Cadence

Slowly lower your forearm back to full extension. Maintain smooth cadence.
